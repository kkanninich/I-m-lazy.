# Community Guidelines

Welcome! These guidelines keep the 7K Re:BIRTH Unlimited Community useful, welcoming, and safe.

## 1. Respect people

- Be friendly to newcomers and experienced members alike.
- Critique ideas and technical work, not people.
- No harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, or targeted abuse.
- Keep discussions constructive and on topic.

## 2. Share responsibly

- Describe whether a claim is tested, experimental, or only a hypothesis.
- Include relevant game version, platform, tool version, and reproduction steps when reporting an issue.
- Credit original authors and link to legitimate sources.
- Do not claim another person's work as your own.

## 3. Security and privacy

Never post passwords, session cookies, authentication tokens, private keys, personal information, or account-recovery details.

The following are not allowed:
- Malware, credential stealers, spyware, or destructive payloads.
- Phishing, account theft, or instructions to compromise another person's account.
- Attacks, disruption, or unauthorized access against official game services or other people's systems.
- Instructions designed to evade security controls for abuse.

If you discover a security issue, do not publish exploit details or sensitive data. Contact the affected service owner through an appropriate private reporting channel.

## 4. Mods, tools, and private environments

- Only modify or test environments you own or have explicit permission to use.
- Clearly label prerequisites, compatibility, known risks, and whether a project is experimental.
- Do not distribute proprietary game files, paid assets, or copyrighted material without permission.
- Do not present unofficial tools or private projects as official products.
- Respect other players' choice to play the unmodified official game.

## 5. No spam or scams

- No referral spam, fake downloads, deceptive offers, or impersonation.
- Do not ask members to send money, credentials, or account access in exchange for promised resources.
- Disclose affiliations and commercial interests.

## 6. Moderation

Content that violates these guidelines may be removed, and repeat or serious violations may result in restricted participation. Repository maintainers may update these guidelines as the community grows.

## 7. Scope and disclaimer

This is an unofficial fan community and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Netmarble or the game's rights holders. Members are responsible for complying with applicable laws, platform terms, and the permissions governing the software and services they use.
