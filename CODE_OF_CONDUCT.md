# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this community to be welcoming, useful, and safe for players, researchers, creators, translators, and newcomers. Participation is based on respect, curiosity, and responsible sharing.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and technical claims without attacking people.
- Explain whether information is verified, partially tested, experimental, or uncertain.
- Credit original authors and link to legitimate sources.
- Welcome beginners and help them understand context.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam, harassment, and personal disputes.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and the permissions governing software and services.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, targeted abuse, or intimidation.
- Publishing personal information, private conversations, credentials, access tokens, or session cookies without permission.
- Scams, impersonation, malicious links, malware, credential theft, or deceptive downloads.
- Instructions or tools intended to gain unauthorized access, disrupt services, or compromise other players.
- Redistributing proprietary game files, paid assets, or copyrighted material without permission.
- Repeated spam, disruptive promotion, or deliberate misinformation.

## Responsible technical discussion

Keep testing to environments and accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to use. Do not target official services or other players. Explain the scope of a test and avoid posting operational details that expose someone else's accounts, systems, or private data.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or block accounts when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement will consider the severity and context of the behavior. Members can use GitHub's report controls to flag content for review.

Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces directly operated by this community.

## Acknowledgement

By participating, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and the [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md).
