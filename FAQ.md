# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is this an official Seven Knights Re:BIRTH community?

No. This is an independent fan community. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Netmarble or the game's rights holders.

## Does this repository provide an unlimited-resource version of the game?

No game client, modified APK, proprietary game asset, account, or server access is distributed by this repository. The community provides a place to discuss ideas, research, guides, tools, and experiences. Any resource must be shared lawfully by someone who has permission to distribute it.

## What does "Unlimited Gameplay" mean here?

It is the name of a discussion area for resource-rich gameplay experiences, progression systems, game mechanics, and related research. A post in that category is not proof that an unlimited-resource feature or modification exists.

## Do I need to be a programmer?

No. Players, researchers, collectors, translators, guide writers, tool creators, server operators, and curious newcomers are welcome.

## Where should I ask a question?

Use the [Questions and Support Q&A category](https://github.com/kkanninich/I-m-lazy./discussions/categories/questions-and-support). For open-ended conversation, use [General](https://github.com/kkanninich/I-m-lazy./discussions/categories/general).

## Where should I report a bug or compatibility problem?

Use the [Bug or compatibility report issue form](https://github.com/kkanninich/I-m-lazy./issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include the relevant versions and steps to reproduce the problem. Never include passwords, access tokens, session cookies, private account details, or personal data.

## How can I submit a tool or mod?

Use the [Share a tool or modification form](https://github.com/kkanninich/I-m-lazy./issues/new?template=tool_submission.yml). Link to the original author or source, explain the tool's purpose, list compatibility details, and state whether you tested it. Do not upload proprietary game files or paid assets without permission.

## How can I share a guide?

Use the [Guide submission form](https://github.com/kkanninich/I-m-lazy./issues/new?template=guide_submission.yml), or post a guide in the [Guides and Tutorials discussion category](https://github.com/kkanninich/I-m-lazy./discussions/categories/guides-and-tutorials).

## Can I test tools on official services or other players' accounts?

Only use software, accounts, servers, and environments that you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Do not attack, disrupt, bypass access controls, harvest credentials, or interfere with official services or other players.

## How do I know whether a resource works?

Check its stated verification status, version, platform, author, and test notes in the [Resource Directory](RESOURCE_DIRECTORY.md). "Experimental" and "Needs review" do not mean tested or recommended. Even a verified result applies only to the reported configuration.

## Who runs this community?

This is a volunteer fan community, not a formal development team. Community participation is open, subject to the guidelines and the rights of the original creators.
